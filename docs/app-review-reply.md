1. Screen recording
The recording starts with launching the app and shows: creating a trip (destination, days, budget, interests), the generated day-by-day itinerary, the trip map and place details, account creation and sign-in (email, Sign in with Apple), and account deletion (avatar menu > Delete account > confirm).

2. Purpose and target audience
JourneyPilot AI is a travel planning app for leisure travelers (solo, couples, friends, families) who want a complete, personalized itinerary without spending hours researching. The user enters a destination, the number of days, a budget and their interests, and the app generates a day-by-day plan with real hotels, restaurants, activities, hidden gems and photo spots, a budget estimate by category, weather and safety tips, and an interactive map. It solves the time-consuming part of trip preparation and gives a ready-to-use, editable plan.

3. How to access the main features
- Launch the app and sign in with the demo account provided in the App Review Information sign-in fields (or Sign in with Apple).
- Tap "Plan my journey" to create a trip. Enter a destination (e.g. "Lisbon"), the number of days, a budget and interests, then generate the trip.
- Open the trip to see the itinerary day by day, the map, the budget and the tips. Places can be opened in Apple Maps or Google Maps.
- Trips are saved to the account and available on all the user's devices.
- Account deletion: avatar menu (top right) > Delete account > confirm. All user data is permanently deleted.
In this version the app is entirely free: there is no paid content or In-App Purchase.

4. External services used
- Supabase (via Lovable Cloud): database, user authentication and backend functions.
- Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In: optional sign-in methods.
- Lovable AI Gateway (hosted large language models): generation of itineraries and travel recommendations.
- Google Places, OpenStreetMap (Nominatim, Photon) and Wikimedia Commons / Wikidata: place information, coordinates and photos.
- OpenStreetMap tiles: interactive trip map.
- Apple Maps / Google Maps: opening a place in the user's maps app (external link).

5. Regional differences
The app works the same way in all regions where it is available. Content is generated for any destination worldwide. The interface is available in English, French, German, Dutch, Arabic and Chinese, depending on the device language.

6. Regulated industry / third-party material
The app does not operate in a regulated industry. It does not sell travel, take bookings or process payments; it only provides planning suggestions and links to public map services. Place data and photos come from the public sources listed above under their respective licenses.
