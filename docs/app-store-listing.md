**Subtitle:** AI Travel Itinerary Planner

**Promotional text:**

**Keywords:**
trip,travel,itinerary,planner,vacation,holiday,city break,hotel,restaurant,guide,weekend,road trip

**Description:**
JourneyPilot AI is your smart travel companion. Enter your destination, the number of days, your budget and what you love: the AI composes a complete trip for you, built around your pace.

A DAY-BY-DAY ITINERARY
Morning, lunch, afternoon, sunset, dinner: every day is organized around your interests, your travel style (budget, standard or luxury) and the intensity you want.

REAL PLACES, CHOSEN FOR YOU
• Hotels and stays that fit your budget
• Restaurants, from local classics to fine dining
• Authentic activities and experiences
• Hidden gems the locals love
• Photo spots, with the best time for the light

A TRANSPARENT BUDGET
Estimates by category (stay, food, transport, activities), daily budget, emergency reserve and money-saving tips.

EVERYTHING TO TRAVEL WITH PEACE OF MIND
• Interactive map of your trip, day by day
• Weather and best time to go
• Safety and transport tips
• Open any place in Apple Maps or Google Maps

A TRIP THAT FEELS LIKE YOU
First visit or not, solo, as a couple, with friends or family, with children, dietary preferences (vegetarian, halal, allergies…) and accessibility needs: JourneyPilot AI takes it all into account.

Available in English, French, German, Dutch, Arabic and Chinese.

Sign in with Apple or Google to find all your trips on every device.
